Sam Lee, CVT

Veterinary Technician · VCA North Atlantic

5 Questions with Sam

1

Why did you choose to build your career at VCA North Atlantic?

I wanted to work somewhere that saw technicians as clinical partners, not just extra hands. Here, my opinion in a treatment plan actually gets asked for — that changes how invested you feel in every case.

2

What does a typical shift look like for you?

A lot of variety — placing catheters, running labwork, monitoring anesthesia, comforting anxious patients (and sometimes anxious pet parents). I'm usually working side-by-side with a doctor, but I own my part of the case.

3

What's the most rewarding part of the job?

Building trust with a scared patient in just a few minutes. There's a moment mid-appointment where they stop shaking and let you help them — that's the whole job, right there.

4

What's something people might not know about you?

I foster kittens on the side, so it's not unusual for me to show up to a shift with cat hair on my scrubs from a completely different set of patients.

5

What would you tell someone considering a technician role here?

Ask about growth opportunities in the interview. I started in general practice and moved into a leadership track within two years because the team actually invested in getting me there.
